⚠ Only if you trust the pages you use it on. A web page can hide instructions meant to steer pagebot. With this on, pagebot clicks, types and picks options on any page without checking with you first.

Get the browser extension

pagebot in your browser’s side panelWorks in Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer. pagebot stays open as you move between pages and tabs, and it can do what the bookmark can’t: go to other pages, open and switch tabs, type and press keys, click anything it sees in a screenshot, and take screenshots without asking you to share the tab.
Download
  1. Unzip pagebot-extension.zip. You get a folder called pagebot-extension.
  2. In a new tab, go to chrome://extensions (in Edge it’s edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode. It’s a switch at the top right (in Edge, in the left column).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the pagebot-extension folder.
  5. Pin pagebot: click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to pagebot (in Edge, the eye).
  6. Click the pagebot button, or press Alt+Shift+P, to open it in the side panel.
  7. Sign in there if it asks. If you use pagebot’s chat page in this browser, you’re already signed in.

Keep the folder: your browser loads pagebot from it. The same limits apply in the extension: it never touches password, payment card or file fields, and it always asks before it goes to another site.
