What it does

📄

Reads the page

Sends the page text once per chat, so follow-ups stay cheap. Anything you highlight is included automatically.

📷

Screenshots

Captures the whole tab, or drag a box over just the part you mean. The panel hides itself first. You can also paste any image.

🌐

Searches the web

Turn on web search when the page isn't enough, and answers come with their sources.

⚡

Haiku or Sonnet

Haiku 5.5 for quick answers, Sonnet 5.5 when it needs more thought. One click to switch.

🎨

Stays out of the way

Drag it anywhere, shrink it to a pill, and pick from nine themes, including see-through Glass.

🔒

You decide who's in

Runs on your own server behind a password or 5-digit passcode. Friends can ask for an account, and you accept them and set their daily limit in /admin.

Install

  1. Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).
  2. Drag the orange 📌 pagebot button above onto it.
  3. On any page, click the bookmark. Click it again to hide it, or press Esc.
  4. Sign in once per site. The owner unlocks with their password or passcode. Everyone else creates an account and waits to be accepted.

Already have pagebot in your bookmarks? Drag the button again to get the latest version.

Can't drag? Paste it in by hand

Create a new bookmark and paste this as its URL. Some sites block overlays; on those, pagebot opens in a small window instead.
