Browser extension

Keep pagebot beside your page.

Download the extension for desktop Chrome, Edge or Brave. Your familiar pagebot chat opens in the browser’s side panel, stays open as you move between pages and tabs, and can do everything on the page that the bookmark can’t.

Set it up once.

  1. Download and extract the ZIP

    Use the download button above, then extract the ZIP into a folder you’ll keep. You get a pagebot-extension folder with manifest.json directly inside.

  2. Open your browser’s extensions page

    Copy this address into the browser’s address bar and press Enter. Turn on Developer mode.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Choose “Load unpacked”

    Select the extracted pagebot-extension folder—the one with manifest.json directly inside it. Choose the folder, not the ZIP.

  4. Pin pagebot and open it on a page

    Find pagebot in your browser’s extensions menu, pin it, then click its toolbar button (or press Alt + Shift + P). Sign in with your usual pagebot account if it asks. Turn on Click and type for me in the + menu to let it use the page. Your account approval, limits and saved chats still apply.

Using the extension

Click the toolbar button or press Alt + Shift + P to open pagebot. A highlighted passage can also be sent through the right-click menu.

pagebot works on the tab that’s showing and follows you from page to page. Clicks, typing and keys ask first unless you choose Don’t ask before clicking. Going to another site, using a tab it didn’t open and running a script always ask.

It never reads or types into password, card or file fields, and it can’t work on the browser’s own pages or extension stores. While it types or presses keys, the browser shows a bar saying pagebot started debugging this browser. That’s expected, and it goes away a few seconds after the step.

What you’re allowing

  • All websites and scripting: read or act on the page that’s showing, only when you ask pagebot to.
  • Tabs: go to addresses and open, switch and close tabs for you.
  • Debugger: real typing, key presses and clicks at a point, attached only for one step at a time.
  • Side panel: keep the chat in the browser’s own panel.
  • Context menu: add Ask pagebot when you select text.
  • Storage: remember what a step is holding, so nothing is left behind if the panel closes.

Everything it runs is in the ZIP, it only talks to this pagebot site, and web pages can’t send it commands. Page actions keep their confirmation prompts.

Keep the extracted folder. To update the extension later, download a fresh ZIP from this site, replace the extracted files, and press Reload for pagebot on the browser’s extensions page. The chat itself continues to use this site’s current version.
